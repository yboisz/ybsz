01 THE STORY

MORE THAN
A COMMUNITY.

Yboisz is a space for people to connect, express themselves and build something beyond ordinary.

Yboisz started in 2021 during the lockdown era as an online boys community — a place to chat, hang out, game and build friendships.

As the community grew, so did the vision. YB evolved beyond its original circle into a creator community built around people, ideas, style and collaboration.

Today, Yboisz exists as a space where people can meet, create, support, connect and move forward together.
